Home/About

Started with one lathe. Still obsessed.

Torvane began in a two-bay garage in Dayton, Ohio. Today 420 machinists, engineers and inspectors build parts that fly, heal and power the world.

01 / Our story

38 years of getting it right.

1988

One lathe, one promise

Founder Ray Torvane opens a two-bay shop with a manual lathe and a promise: every part ships right, or it ships again at our cost.

1996

First CNC cell & ISO 9001

Our first CNC machining centers arrive, along with ISO 9001 certification and our first aerospace customer.

2008

Fabrication & welding

Sheet metal and certified welding move in-house, letting customers consolidate entire assemblies with one supplier.

2017

Medical & metrology lab

ISO 13485 certification, a clean room, and a climate-controlled metrology lab with six CMMs.

2024

Automation & 24-hour quotes

Lights-out robotic cells and an engineer-reviewed 24-hour quoting promise. Now serving 1,200+ engineering teams.

02 / The facility

180,000 sq ft of capacity.

Two buildings, three shifts, one quality system. Customers are welcome to visit any time; most leave with a new part number for us.

03 / What we believe

Three rules we don't bend.

01

Say it, ship it

We quote real lead times and hit them. 99.7% of the time, anyway, and we tell you early when we won't.

02

Engineers answer

Every customer has a named manufacturing engineer and program manager. No ticket queues.

03

Own the miss

Out-of-spec parts are remade at our cost and expedited. No disputes, no restocking fees.
