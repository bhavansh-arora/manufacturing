Quality at every step

Five checkpoints before it ships.

Defects are cheapest to catch early, so inspection isn't a single gate at the end. It runs through the whole order.

  1. Contract reviewEngineering and quality review every drawing note, spec and flow-down before we accept the PO.
  2. Incoming materialMill certs verified against spec, material identity checked and lot numbers logged for traceability.
  3. First articleThe first part off every new setup is fully measured and signed off before the run continues.
  4. In-process SPCMachine probing and operator checks on critical features at defined intervals, charted in real time.
  5. Final inspectionSample or 100% inspection to your AQL, then visual, packaging and paperwork checks.
